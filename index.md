# I work on neuromorphic control: feedback loops that regulate with spikes instead of smooth signals.

![Portrait of Christian Fernandez Lorden]({{ '/assets/img/me.jpg' | relative_url }}){: .portrait}

I am a PhD student at the [Neuroengineering lab](https://www.neuroengineering.uliege.be) ([Montefiore Institute](https://www.montefiore.uliege.be/)) of the [University of Liège](https://www.uliege.be/). My thesis asks what control theory can say about neuromorphic primitives, and what those primitives can do inside a control loop.
