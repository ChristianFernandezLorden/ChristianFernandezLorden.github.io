I am a PhD student at the Montefiore Institute, in the Faculty of Applied Sciences of the University of Liège, Belgium. I work on neuromorphic engineering applied to control and regulation systems.

My background is in electronics, control theory and dynamical systems. I like problems where a proof and real-life have to agree.

## Teaching

I am a teaching assistant for the databases ([Base de données](https://www.programmes.uliege.be/cocoon/20262027/cours/INFO0009-2.html)) and probability ([Eléments du calcul des probabilités](https://www.programmes.uliege.be/cocoon/20262027/cours/MATH0062-1.html)) courses at the University of Liège.
