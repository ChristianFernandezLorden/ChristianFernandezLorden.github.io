Neurons regulate with spikes, brief all-or-nothing events, instead of smooth signals. I study systems that work the same way, with the tools of nonlinear systems and control theory.

- **Excitable systems.** What makes a system spike and how to analyse it.
- **Impulsive and event-based feedback.** Controllers that act through short pulses, and how to prove that the closed loop is stable.
